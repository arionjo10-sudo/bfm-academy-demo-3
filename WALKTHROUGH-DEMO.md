# Dry-run walkthrough (demo)

1. Open index.html. A black DEMO bar sits on top.
2. Director: open Admin, enter DEMO1-TRYIT. Check Overview (50 students, 24 pending requests, items to grade, 4 messages).
3. Students: search, filter by ministry, open the pastor (Pastor Elias Montgomery: every course, all 9 credentials). Open any student to see fun facts, favorite scripture, started courses and written work.
4. Review & grade: grade a project, then see it in Recent activity and Reports.
5. Requests: approve and decline a few (skip a course, Track A, Teacher Certification, Discipleship, another ministry). Approved ones disappear from the list.
6. Messages: reply to one of the 4 unanswered questions.
7. First open: the "Welcome to the demo" announcement appears centered over a dark blurred background; close it with the X. The demo starts with just that one announcement. Announcements and Events: Announcements tab -> New announcement. Pick Inline or Floating, an icon, colors, start and end date and time. Post two or three and view as a student: the floating pop-up shows "1 of 3" with arrows and an X. Archive one and see it under Past announcements on the News page. Add an Event: students then get a "new events" pop-up that scrolls to the footer calendar.
8. Reports: switch Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly; download the PDF and CSV.
9. Student view: use "View as" to open the Academy as the pastor, a brand-new student, a class student, and a Track 3 leader. Check Next lesson, the tour, certificates, and that grades you gave appear.
10. Class gating: View as a Church member who hasn't finished classes: Track 1-3 are locked and the class pages offer "Request to skip" for New Believers and Water Baptism. Demo leaders already finished the classes. To see the leader prompt, View as a Church member (for example Terrence Taylor), open Profile, set the position to Deacon and save, then open a class page: "Would you like to skip this class?" with Yes and No. Yes shows "Awaiting approval from the Academy"; approve it as director under Requests.
11. Back to "Director (my view)". Use "Reset demo data" to start over.

What is made up: all names, ministries, answers, grades and requests. Dates are set around October 26, 2026.
